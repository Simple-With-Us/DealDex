## What changed

- Settings renders. Encryption helpers moved to `src/lib/server/desk-keys.server.ts` so the browser bundle no longer imports `node:crypto`.
- Scan button is title case (`Scan Market` / `Scanning…`) on web, iOS, and Android. The lightning bolt is gone.
- Filter values are short so they are not clipped on a phone. Selects, quick views, clear, row actions, and the menu are at least 44px tall.

- User-facing “desk”, “dossier”, “SCAN”, and “API desks” copy is plain English: price sources, card details, and price range.
- iOS camera control and the save control are 44pt. Android and iOS subtitles match the website comparison line.
- Error screen no longer prints the raw module exception.
